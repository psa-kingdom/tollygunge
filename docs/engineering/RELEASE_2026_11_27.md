## Member onboarding

Members → Bulk onboarding → **New Free members — invitations held** accepts a
CSV or XLSX with `name,email,phone`. Name and email are required; phone is optional
and validated when supplied. Missing professional/profile fields can be completed
after sign-in. New records atomically receive an active Free membership, an
unverified profile, a saved application draft, and a held invitation. Importing
does not grant professional verification or payment approval. Existing email
accounts are skipped, duplicate rows are reported, and repeating a committed
batch does not recreate accounts.

In the directory, select the imported members, review the approved invitation
template and recipient list, and confirm sending. Each recipient gets a separate
single-use, 48-hour password setup link. After setup, they sign in and complete
the saved profile. The import itself does not send emails.

The Membership type dropdown in the member-details view assigns Free, Life, or
Patron. Administrators can change existing plans, including for directory members
without login accounts. The operation checks the record version, clears an old
pending plan request, synchronizes the linked account/application, and records an
audit entry. It does not change membership status, mark payments received, or
verify professional credentials.
