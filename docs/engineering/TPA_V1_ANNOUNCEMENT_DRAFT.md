# General announcement — release draft, do not send before hosted acceptance

Dear TPA community,

A new home for our association is ready to welcome you. The TPA website brings our people, activities and knowledge together, with a member portal that keeps the practical work close at hand.

You can explore association information, published events and resources, contact the secretariat, and manage your own account, profile, documents and inquiries. Members can follow their application and verification steps; authorized staff have dedicated tools for reviews, communications, publishing, events and manual payment records. Email verification, profile review, membership and payment status remain separate, so every step has a clear meaning.

Member invitations will be sent by the administrator when the association is ready. The existing 202 invitations are safely held until that action. When your invitation arrives, follow its access steps, verify your email and complete the details requested in your portal. Existing users can sign in at https://tpassociation.org/login and use password recovery if needed. Please never share a password or recovery link.

Something feel confusing or fail to work? Choose **Report an issue** from your account menu or the website footer. Tell us what you expected, what happened and how to reproduce it. You can preview and optionally include basic technical context. Keep the reference shown after submission; signed-in members can follow their inquiry's status. Please keep passwords, identity documents and financial details out of issue reports.

This first release starts an ongoing service: we will address reported defects, refine accessibility and performance, and improve workflows based on how members actually use them. Online gateway payments and unapproved automated communications remain inactive.

Thank you for helping make this a useful, welcoming space for the TPA community.
